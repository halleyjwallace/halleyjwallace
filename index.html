About

A recent graduate with a B.S. in Biomedical Engineering, with a strong interest in experimental fluid mechanics, bio-inspired design, and propulsion systems. My undergraduate research at the Bhamla Lab at Georgia Tech centered on a DARPA squid-inspired superpropulsion project, spanning design and fabrication to open-water experimental validation and high-speed imaging. Graduated in December 2025 with a minor in Chinese, including a semester abroad in Shenzhen as a U.S. Department of State Gilman Scholar.

This Fall 2026, attending Sunny Jung's Bio-Inspired Fluids Lab at Cornell University for graduate school, fully funded. Outside of research, I served as Vice President of Georgia Tech's Medical Robotics Club and led capstone work developing a reusable medical device for hospitals in resource-limited settings.
